Nextspace

Privacy policy

Last updated 31 October 2026 · Operated by Nextern Consultancy for its own team at mail.nexternconsultancy.com

Nextspace (formerly AliasMail) is the private webmail used by Nextern Consultancy staff to read and send email for their company addresses. It is not a public service. This page explains what it stores and shares.

What Nextspace stores

  • Email of the company mailbox: message details and copies needed to show, search and send mail.
  • Accounts: each person's company address, name, profile picture, signature, encrypted password and 2-Step Verification settings.
  • Sign-in records: device and browser, IP address, approximate location, and when sign-ins and security changes happened.
  • Google sign-in, if a person chooses to connect it: Google's account ID, email address and name, used only to recognise that person when they sign in with Google.
  • Google apps, if a person connects Drive, Meet or Chat: an encrypted Google access token, used only for the actions described below and deleted when they disconnect.

What is shared with others

  • Email delivery: messages go through the company's email provider (GoDaddy), as with any mail app.
  • Approximate location: a sign-in's IP address is looked up once a day at ipapi.co to show a city and internet provider. A person who shares their device location sends only rounded coordinates to OpenStreetMap to get a town name; the coordinates are not stored.
  • Sign in with Google: only basic profile information (account ID, email address, name) is received from Google.
  • Google Drive (optional): Nextspace can open only the files a person picks with Google's file picker, and shares them with an email's recipients when that person sends it. It cannot see the rest of their Drive.
  • Google Meet / Calendar (optional): Nextspace creates the meetings a person asks for as events in their own Google Calendar; Google sends the invitations. It does not read or change their other events.
  • Google Chat (optional): Nextspace lists a person's Chat spaces and creates the spaces they ask for, with the people they choose. It does not read their messages.
  • Nextspace never reads Gmail or Google contacts. Data received from Google is used only for these features, is not shared with anyone else, and is not used for advertising or to train AI models.

Nothing is sold or used for advertising.

Who can see it

Each person sees their own mailbox and the shared mailboxes their role gives them. The company's administrator can see the whole mailbox and the sign-in records, to run and secure the service.

Keeping and removing data

Data is kept while a person works with the company. When an account is removed, its sign-ins and Google connection end at once; email that belongs to the company mailbox stays with the company. A person can disconnect Google at any time in Settings → Security, and Drive, Meet or Chat in Settings → Google apps.
